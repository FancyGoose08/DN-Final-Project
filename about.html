About Us

Meet the Everett Digital Navigators!

Destiny Martinez

Lead Everett Digital Navigator. Destiny has been working with seniors as a Digital Navigator for 2 years, providing one-on-one tech support and training to help them access healthcare portals, telehealth, and digital health tools. She is passionate about bridging the digital divide and empowering seniors to navigate the digital world with confidence.

Thomas Guerrero

Everett Digital Navigator and Youth Development and Enrichment Intern. Thomas is a dedicated Digital Navigator with a background in community outreach and youth development. His skills in communication and relationship-building have been instrumental in recruiting high school volunteers for our intergenerational tech support club, fostering meaningful connections between students and seniors in Everett.

Gustavo Aguiar

Everett Digital Navigator and lead full-stack developer for this website. Gustavo is a new Digital Navigator, but has been passionate about technology and web development for years. He is excited to use his skills to help seniors and other community members in Everett navigate the digital world.
