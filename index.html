Everett Digital Navigator Team
Digital Inclusion Solution

Connecting Generations to Close Everett's Digital Divide

Older adults in Everett are increasingly required to navigate online healthcare systems without adequate support. Our program trains local high school students — with the help of MACIR's Digital Navigators — to serve as digital mentors, helping seniors gain the confidence and skills needed to access essential healthcare services.

Who are the Digital Navigators?

A Digital Navigator is someone who helps people access and use technology in their daily loves. DNs provide one-on-one support to help individuals with:

  • Tech Support

  • Access to free or low-cost devices

  • Filing out online forms

  • Referrals to digital skills classes

  • Sharing info about community resources

Digital Navigators work under MACIR to help bridge the digital devide in their communities.

Our Problem

Our problem statement is as follows:

As essential services increasingly move online, older adults aged 55+ in Everett with limited digital independence face digital equity barriers to accessing healthcare. The absence of accessible and ongoing digital support creates structural barriers that delay care, undermine financial security, and limit economic participation.

Our Solution

Our solution creates an intergenerational after-school tech support club where high school students are trained to navigate healthcare portals and digital health tools, then provide ongoing, hands-on support to seniors in Everett. By pairing student volunteers with seniors, we build digital independence, improve access to care, and strengthen community connections.
