Solution

Our Solution

Our solution creates an intergenerational after-school tech support club where high school students are trained to navigate healthcare portals and digital health tools, then provide ongoing, hands-on support to seniors in Everett. By pairing student volunteers with seniors, we build digital independence, improve access to care, and strengthen community connections.

Logic Model of the Solution

Inputs

The "inputs" are resources that we need to get our solution off the ground. They consist of:

  • A device for each of the volunteers
  • Finances to pay for the devices
  • Software Engineers to build and maintain the "Intergen Nexus" website where seniors can request help and volunteers can track their hours and tickets
  • Intergen Nexus website
  • Partnering with the Everett High School's Careers & Technical Education (C&TE)'s IT and STEM courses
  • Partnering with senior centers and areas where senior events are held
  • After school club members to act as volunteers
  • A supervisor or DN to manage the club and teach the volunteers how to use the healthcare portals
  • A space to hold meeting and teach the volunteers
  • and an outreach program to make the right group of people aware of the program

Activities

The "activities" are what we will be doing with the inputs to reach the desired outputs. The activities consist of:

  • Recruit and train volunteers how to use eHealth portals with devices
  • Develop and maintain "Intergen Nexus" website
  • Conduct outreach to seniors and their families to make them aware of the program
  • Receive and review help requests from seniors and their families through the website
  • Match seniors with appropriate volunteers based on their needs and the volunteers' expertise
  • Write tickets for help requests and track them through resolution
  • Track and verify service hours for volunteers
  • Hold regular meetings to provide ongoing training and support for volunteers
  • Monitor safety protocols to ensure the well-being of both seniors and volunteers
  • Collect feedback from seniors and volunteers to improve the program

Outputs

The "outputs" are the immediate results of the activites. The outputs are:

  • Outreach is developed and awareness is made
  • Volunteers are trained on how to use eHealth portals and how to communicate with seniors
  • Seniors get their appointments booked
  • Seniors get acquainted with volunteers and eHealth portals
  • High schoolers get volunteer hours they can use in college applications and experience for future jobs
  • High schoolers develop tech support and customer support skills while assisting seniors

Outcome

After all is said and done, seniors receive digital support — allowing them to access healthcare and build their confidence with using tech. A side effect of this is that high schoolers who volunteered gain volunteer hours and experience that they can use in college applications and future jobs.

This is our proposed solution for solving this problem.
